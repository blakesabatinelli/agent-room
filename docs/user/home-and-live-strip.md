# The home, the live strip, and Advanced

Agent Room opens on a calm home, not a wall of settings. The point is to make the everyday thing easy and keep the rest out of the way.

## The home

- **Greeting and a goal box.** Type what you want done in "What do you want to get done?" and press Start (or Return on a keyboard). It starts a goal in the room you last worked in, or, if you have no rooms yet, offers to make one. One step.
- **In progress.** Any running or waiting goals, each with its room, its state (Running, Waiting on you, …) and how many of its requirements are met. Tap one to open its room.
- **Rooms.** Your rooms as cards, each with a one-line what's-happening: the live goal, who is working, whether there is something new, or the room's topic. **New room** is here.
- **Agents.** Your agents, as direct-message cards.

## The live strip

A slim line above the home and every conversation. It is glanceable, not a dashboard:

- **What is running** right now (agent and task), with a quiet progress dot.
- **Approvals waiting** as a single tap to review.
- **Usage left** per agent, as a small chip with a coloured dot: green for plenty, amber for getting low, red for almost out or out, and plain for unlimited (a local or free agent) or while the hub is still learning your limits. Tap a chip to see that agent's 5-hour and weekly windows per subscription.

When nothing is happening the strip is just the row of usage chips.

## Advanced

The everyday nav is Home, the conversation, Agents and Approvals. Everything else lives behind one **Advanced** entry: Memory, Permissions, Folders, Control files, Subscriptions, Remote access, Personas, Résumés, Scorecard, Approval rules, Office, Issues and Appearance. Nothing was removed; it is one tap further in. Approvals stays one tap away with a count whenever any is pending, and ⌘K (Ctrl-K) still jumps to any screen by name.

## Deleting a room

A room has a quiet **Delete room** action: on the phone it is at the bottom of the room's info screen; on the Mac it is in the room's settings. It asks you to confirm, then removes the room and everything in it. If work is still running, Agent Room says so and offers **Stop and delete**, which stops the work first. The last channel cannot be deleted.
